Pattern bank

Ranked by hits on the seven papers (both sittings of Jan20r/Jan22r count). Trap first; method second.

Block 1 · probability

$$P(B\mid A)=\frac{P(A\mid B)P(B)}{P(A\mid B)P(B)+P(A\mid B^c)P(B^c)}$$
P1 · 5 Bayes with a complement / base-rate. Jan20 Q2 · Pr21 1a · Jan20r/22r 1a · Nov22 1a. Trap: reporting sensitivity or \(P(A)\) instead of \(P(B\mid A)\). Method: name events, write Bayes with \(B^c\), compute the denominator.
P16 · 4 Valid ACF / WSS / ergodicity / discrete vs continuous. Jan19 1a · Jan20 1a · Jan20r/22r 1bc · Nov22 1c. ACF tests, in order: even; \(r[0]\ge|r[\ell]|\); \(P(e^{j\theta})\ge 0\). Random constant \(X(t)=A\) is WSS, not ergodic. Pixel 0–255 is a discrete RV; a whole picture is one realization.
P19 · 1 Joint PMF → marginal. Nov22 1b. Sum the other variable. Trap: treating \(Y\)’s support as \(X\)’s.
P20 · 1 Discrete PMF plug-in (binomial / geometric). Pr21 1d. If the PMF is printed, plug in. Do not assume it will be given (Pr21 only). Pascal on that sheet has a typesetting error.
P14 · 2 Spectral factorization / min-phase \(L(z)\). Jan20r/22r 1d · Nov22 1d. Write \(P\) in \(\cos\theta\), replace \(\cos\theta=(z+z^{-1})/2\), put poles and zeros inside the unit circle into \(L\). Trap: \(L(z^{-1})\) is max-phase; \(\sigma_i^2\) vs \(1/\sigma_i^2\); printed sign error (Jan22r 1d).

Block 2 · estimation

P9 · 4 MLE from an iid PDF. Jan19 2a exp rate · Nov19 3a Rayleigh \(\theta\) · Pr21 2b Rayleigh \(\theta=\sigma^2\) · Nov22 2d angle. Trap: parameterization. \((x/\theta^2)\exp(-x^2/(2\theta^2))\) has a square root; \((x/\theta)\exp(-x^2/(2\theta))\) does not. Method: \(\ln\prod p\), \(\partial/\partial\theta=0\).
P10 · 4 CRLB, Fisher, regularity. Nov19 3b · Pr21 2a · Jan20r/22r 2a+2e · Nov22 2a+2e. \(\mathcal I(\theta)=-E[\partial^2\ln p/\partial\theta^2]\), \(\mathrm{var}\ge 1/\mathcal I\), \(N\) iid \(\Rightarrow\times N\). Efficient iff \(\partial\ln p/\partial\theta=\mathcal I(\theta)(g-\theta)\). Trap: if the support depends on \(\theta\) (Jan22r 2e, \(p=2x/\theta\) on \([0,\theta]\)) the CRLB does not exist. Do not grind Fisher.
P11 · 4 MAP vs MMSE. Jan19 2bc · Nov19 3c · Jan20r/22r 2f–h · Nov22 2fg. MMSE \(=E[\theta\mid x]\) (mean); MAP \(=\arg\max p(\theta\mid x)\) (mode). BPSK in WGN: \(\tanh(y/\sigma^2)\) and \(\mathrm{sign}(y)\) — identity usually printed. Gamma(2, \(\beta\)): mean \(2/\beta\), mode \(1/\beta\). Trap: “MMSE is the maximum of the posterior”.
P17 · 1 LS / Gauss–Newton, nonlinear in a parameter. Jan20 Q4. Noise PDF unknown \(\Rightarrow\) LS. Linear: \(\hat\theta=(H^\top H)^{-1}H^\top x\). Nonlinear: Gauss–Newton (Newton–Raphson also allowed). Jan22r 2c “some NL-LS separate into linear” — the 2020 key already says that is no longer in the content.
P18 · 1 Efficient-estimator inversion. Pr21 2a only. Integrate \(\partial\ln p/\partial\theta=\mathcal I(\theta)(\bar x-\theta)\) and normalize. Hint on the paper is complete-the-square. They stop at the law of \(\bar x\), not the full \(N\)-sample Gaussian.

Block 3 · spectra

$$a_1=-\frac{r[1]}{r[0]},\quad\sigma_i^2=r[0]+a_1 r[1],\quad P_{\mathrm{AR}}(e^{j\theta})=\sigma_i^2\bigl|1+a_1 e^{-j\theta}\bigr|^{-2}$$

Course convention: \(x[n]+a_1 x[n-1]+\cdots=i[n]\). Wiener 1-step is the same thing, \(w_{\mathrm{opt}}=-a_1\). Correlogram: \(P_{\mathrm{cor}}=r[0]+2\sum_k r[k]\cos(k\theta)\).

P2 · 5 Correlogram from a given ACF, in \(\cos(k\theta)\), sketch \([-\pi,\pi]\). Nov19 2a · Jan20 3a · Pr21 3c · Jan20r/22r 3c · Nov22 3g. Trap: truncated ACF \(\Rightarrow\) PSD can go negative (Nov19 blue curve). Quote min/max at \(0,\pi\). Only use the lags the model allows (MA(\(q\)) \(\Rightarrow |k|\le q\)).
P3 · 5 AR(\(p\)) Yule–Walker + PSD in \(\cos\theta\) + overlay. Nov19 2b AR2 · Pr21 3b AR1 · Jan20r/22r 3d · Nov22 3f. Trap: \(1+a_1 e^{-j\theta}\) vs \(1-a_1 e^{-j\theta}\); using the fallback ACF with the wrong sign of \(r[1]\) (Pr21). Negative \(r[1]\) \(\Rightarrow\) high-pass (dip at 0).
P12 · 4 MA(\(q\)) from ACF; PSD = correlogram. Jan20 3b · Pr21 3c · Jan20r/22r 3b–c · Nov22 3g. \(r[q]=\sigma_i^2 b_q\), \(r[0]=\sigma_i^2(1+\sum b_k^2)\). Trap: two real roots — keep min-phase \(|b|<1\). Fallback \((a,b)\) on the paper may be invalid (Jan22r \(a=\tfrac14,b=\tfrac54\)).
P13 · 2 ARMA(1,1) from a DE / \(H(z)\): ACF, then fake AR1 and MA1. Pr21 3 · Nov22 3c–g. Expand the DE for \(r[0],r[1]\); AR tail for \(|\tau|>q\); AR(1) = YW on \((r[0],r[1])\); MA(1) = correlogram of \(\{0,\pm1\}\). Sketch values at \(0,\pi/2,\pi\).
P15 · 4 Nonparametric SE MC (periodogram / Bartlett / Welch / BT, windows, zeropad). Jan19 1c · Nov19 1c · Jan20 1bc · Nov22 3ab. Periodogram: as. unbiased, var \(\propto P^2\), inconsistent. Bartlett \(K\) non-overlap: var \(\propto 1/K\), bias up. Welch at the same \(K\): var larger than Bartlett, not \(\propto 1/K\) (Jan20 1b official D). Mainlobe = resolution, sidelobes = leakage. Zeropad interpolates; it does not raise resolution.
P21 · 1 FTD vs DFT vs zeropad association plot. Jan20r/22r 3a (two sittings). Infinite cosine \(\to\) two impulses; window \(\to\) sinc; DFT = samples of that sinc; zeropad = denser samples, same envelope.

Block 4 · detection

$$\mathcal L=\frac{p(x\mid\mathcal H_1)}{p(x\mid\mathcal H_0)}\gtrless\gamma$$

NP: max \(P_D\) at fixed \(P_{\mathrm{FA}}\). Bayes 0-1: \(\gamma=P_0/P_1\). \(P_M=1-P_D\). Jan19 published the ratio upside down — use this convention.

P4 · 7 LRT + shade \(P_D/P_{\mathrm{FA}}/P_M\) on a PDF plot. Every paper. Trap: which region is “decide H1”. Heavier tails / larger variance \(\Rightarrow\) outer tails. Faster exponential / narrower Laplace \(\Rightarrow\) centre / small \(|x|\), and the inequality in \(\sum T(x_n)\gtrless\gamma'\) flips. Shade FA on H0 over the H1 set; miss is the complement on H1.
P5 · 3 Mean-shift Gaussian + erf table \(\to P_d,P_{fa}\) (+ ROC). Jan19 3b · Pr21 4a. \(P(y>y_{\mathrm{th}})=\tfrac12[1-\mathrm{erf}((y_{\mathrm{th}}-\mu)/(\sigma\sqrt2))]\). \(P_{fa}\) uses \(\mu=0\). Trap: using the CDF (left tail) inverts the ROC into the lower triangle. Larger \(|\mu|\) \(\Rightarrow\) steeper, upper triangle.
P6 · 2 Equal-mean unequal-variance Gaussians (energy detector). Nov19 4 · Pr21 4b (log-LRT only). Statistic \(\sum x^2[n]\). H1 in the tails if \(\sigma_1>\sigma_0\). \(\ln(\sigma_0^N/\sigma_1^N)-\sum x^2/(2\sigma_1^2)+\sum x^2/(2\sigma_0^2)\gtrless\ln\gamma\).
P7 · 4 Two exponentials / Laplace / folded-exp, \(\mathcal L=1\) threshold. Jan20 5 · Jan20r/22r 4 · Nov22 4. Set \(p_1/p_0=1\), solve \(x_{\mathrm{th}}\). Trap: \(\int_{-\infty}^{x_{\mathrm{th}}}\) vs \(\int_{x_{\mathrm{th}}}^\infty\). Jan20 hint “design so \(P_d>P_{fa}\)” \(\Rightarrow\) left tail for the faster exponential.
P8 · 3 ROC operating-point arithmetic. Jan20 5bc · Pr21 4a · Nov22 4e–g. Bayesian threshold is not \(\mathcal L=1\); it is \(P_0/P_1\) (0-1 costs). Nov22 4f plots the two weighted PDFs.
P22 · 1 Matched filter MC. Jan20r/22r 4a. True: output SNR maximised. Impulse response is \(s[-n]\), not \(s[n]\). Exists for coloured noise too, after whitening.

Key errata

Jan19 3a: the published LRT is inverted — \(\mathcal L=p(\mathcal H_0)/p(\mathcal H_1)\). Later papers and SLT7 use \(p(\mathcal H_1)/p(\mathcal H_0)\). Write the standard ratio; flag the key if you quote Jan19.

Nov19 1a: exam-with-answers says B; oldexams says no option is correct (B is the sequence lagged / reversed). Direct \(r_{xy}[\tau]=\frac1N\sum x[n]y[n+\tau]\) matches shifted-B. Prefer oldexams.

Jan19 2b: published MMSE is the one-sample formula despite \(N\) in 2a. The \(N\)-sample version is a truncated \(\mathrm{Gamma}(N+1,S)\), \(S=\sum x_i\).

Nov19 3b: the key writes \(\mathrm{VAR}(\hat\theta)\ge\hat\theta^2/(4N)\) — it plugged the estimate. The bound is \(\theta^2/(4N)\).

Jan19 2c: MAP omits the projection onto \([0,2]\). Uniform prior is flat on \((0,2)\), so MAP = MLE only if the unconstrained MLE sits inside; otherwise \(\hat\lambda=2\).
